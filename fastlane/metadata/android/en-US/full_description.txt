XGesture is a universal system-level gesture and single-handed productivity suite designed for Android devices.

Key Features:
- Customizable edge triggers (multi-angle swipes, long swipes, double taps)
- Floating ball, ring launcher, and corner wheel quick menus
- Quick tools, notification timeline, and widget panels
- Reachability floating pointer for easy one-handed large-screen operation
- 50+ system actions: brightness, volume, flashlight, lock screen, screenshot, OCR
- Accessibility & Shizuku integration for advanced gestures
- Privacy-friendly: 100% open-source with no ads or proprietary tracking SDKs
