Hey XDA,

I wanted to share **XGesture**, a system-level gesture and productivity tool I've been developing. If you've missed Samsung's **One Hand Operation+ (OHO+)** after switching to Pixel, Xiaomi, Motorola, or Sony, or if you've been searching for an open-source, subscription-free alternative to **Quick Cursor** and **FooView**, this is built for you.

---

### 🌟 Key Highlights

* **Samsung OHO+ Gestures for Non-Samsung Phones**:
  * Edge trigger bars with independent angle recognition (straight swipe, diagonal up/down, long-swipe holds).
  * 50+ configurable actions: system navigation, volume/brightness expansion, clipboard history, quick launch panels, and OEM small windows.
  * Dedicated landscape triggers so gaming or watching videos isn't interrupted.

* **Reachability Pointer (Quick Cursor style)**:
  * Swipe inwards to summon a floating cursor, letting your thumb easily reach any corner of the screen without repositioning your hand.

* **Floating Ball & Local Offline OCR / Image Search**:
  * Drag-to-select text from any app using accessibility nodes or offline **ML Kit / Tesseract / PaddleOCR ONNX** fallback.
  * Aggregated multi-engine reverse image search (Google, Yandex, SauceNAO, TinEye, trace.moe).

* **XDA Geeks & Power-User Features**:
  * **Shizuku & Root Integration**: Execute shell commands, launch non-exported activities, and bypass aggressive OEM background killers.
  * **Optional LSPosed Module**: Hook-level background clipboard listening on Android 10+ and SMS OTP extraction.
  * **Freeform Window Support**: Native Android Freeform, Xiaomi MIUI/HyperOS small windows, and Meizu Flyme small windows.

* **Design & Performance**:
  * Built using Kotlin 2.4, Jetpack Compose, and Miuix design language with real-time progressive Gaussian blur and haptic feedback.
  * Completely open source (AGPL-3.0), zero trackers, zero ads.
